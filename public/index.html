01 / Project brief

Turn lead follow-up into an operating system.

Property enquiries can arrive from several places while teams manage calls, chats, visits, and callbacks separately. This project maps a unified journey: capture the lead once, respond quickly, qualify with consistent criteria, and route the next action without losing context.

Problem to solve

Fragmented intake and inconsistent follow-up can leave enquiries delayed, duplicated, or without a clear owner. The screenshots establish the intended flow; baseline performance has not been supplied.

Target experience

Every enquiry should have a source, current status, conversation history, next action, and accountable owner. Automation handles repeatable steps; sales handles high-intent and nuanced conversations.

Design principle

Automate coordination and reminders, not business judgment. Recheck lead state before acting, keep a human handoff available, and measure results before claiming impact.

02 / End-to-end workflow

One lead record. Clear next step.

The workflow consolidates the screenshot flow into five operating stages. The routing rules below are documented as a design, not a live automation.

A · Capture

Collect, normalize, and identify

Receive enquiries from paid social lead forms, the property website, and Google Ads. Normalize contact details, retain attribution, and check for an existing record before creating a lead.

Meta lead formsWebsite formGoogle AdsDuplicate check · recommended
B · Respond

Attempt a timely first call

For an eligible new lead, the proposed calling agent checks the shared record and attempts the first call within one minute. It uses Hindi and asks the team's configured questions. Already-booked leads are skipped.

First attempt ≤ 1 min · targetHindiSkip booked
C · Qualify

Capture needs and write back

Ask about project, budget, size, own use or investment, and timeline. Save the conversation summary, call result, lead score, and next action. The scoring rubric still needs business definition.

NeedBudgetSizeIntentTimeline
D · Converse

Continue on WhatsApp with context

The proposed WhatsApp agent reads the lead's full history and replies in Hindi or English. It can answer approved questions, share approved materials, book a visit, or hand off price discussions. A 10–20 second reply is the source design target, not verified performance.

Full lead historyHindi / English30-min call hold during chatHuman handoff
E · Measure

Follow up, alert, and learn

Route by Hot/Warm/Cold status, log each action, surface failures, and send a daily 1 PM leads/calls/visits report. Review operational and conversion metrics before tuning the system.

Status-based sequenceError alert1 PM reportOutcome review

HOT · Sales owns it

A booked visit or near-term intent (within 30 days) triggers a same-day sales alert. Stop general nurture and transfer ownership to the sales team.

WARM · Scheduled nurture

Interest is 1–3 months away. Proposed WhatsApp touches on days 2, 5, and 10, plus a call on the lead's scheduled follow-up date.

COLD · Low-frequency or stop

No answer after three attempts, not interested, or not ready. Stop calls. The source suggests one monthly message and possible team revival, subject to channel eligibility.

04 / Reliability and trust

Control the failure paths, not just the happy path.

These operational safeguards supplement the screenshot requirements. They are design recommendations until implemented and tested against the selected providers.

State check before every action

Re-read the latest lead status, owner, booking, opt-out, attempt count, and hold before sending a queued call or message.

Deduplication and safe retries

Use a stable lead identifier and idempotent actions to avoid duplicate rows, repeated messages, and multiple bookings after timeouts.

Bounded calls and chat hold

Cap call attempts at three, space unanswered retries by two hours, and suppress calls during the 30-minute active-chat hold.

Human ownership is explicit

Sales owns Hot leads, negotiated pricing, and requested handoffs. Cancel automation after booking or accepted human ownership.

Consent and suppression

Honor recorded channel preferences and opt-outs across call and messaging paths. Verify eligibility before applying any Cold re-engagement.

Failure alert plus recovery

Alert an operator when a step fails, preserve enough context to investigate, and make unresolved work visible rather than silently dropping it.

05 / Build roadmap

A credible path from design to measured pilot.

The next build should prove one narrow, observable journey first. Expand only after the end-to-end path and stop conditions behave reliably.

PHASE 0 · FOUNDATION

Confirm operating rules

Agree lead fields, scoring rubric, ownership, consent, approved content, visit booking rules, time zone, and escalation contacts.

PHASE 1 · MVP INTAKE

One source → one record

Connect one lead source to n8n and the lead store. Add normalization, duplicate handling, status checks, logs, and a human-visible test path.

PHASE 2 · ASSISTED CONTACT

Call and review

Integrate one calling provider. Start with human review of summaries and outcomes; verify language, retry limits, state checks, and failure alerts.

PHASE 3 · MESSAGING PILOT

WhatsApp and nurture

Connect an approved WhatsApp provider, add the chat hold and handoff, test Hot/Warm/Cold paths, and measure contact and visit outcomes.

Go/no-go gate: do not automate broader outreach until permissions, duplicate protection, opt-out suppression, handoff, and event logging pass real test cases.
