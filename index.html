Operating guide · 15 steps

What this workflow does, step by step

Follow the lead from first enquiry to a clear next action. Expand each step for its trigger, operating rule, record update, and handoff.

01Bring enquiries into one queue

Meta lead forms, the website form at omshivbuildvision.com, and Google Ads enquiries enter the shared Google Sheet. Capture available contact details, source, submission time, and communication preference. Preserve the source for channel reporting, then pass the lead to intake checks.

02Normalize and deduplicate

Normalize phone numbers and search for an existing lead before creating a row. Append new enquiries to the existing history so repeat submissions do not trigger parallel calls or messages. A stable lead ID and retry-safe intake are recommended; the screenshots do not define the deduplication rule.

03Keep one current lead record

Use the sheet as the shared source of truth for contact details, source, status, temperature/score, attempt count and results, conversation summary, visit, active hold, next follow-up, owner, and last-updated time. Read the latest row before acting and write the outcome and timestamp after acting.

04Check eligibility before outreach

Before a due call or message, check whether the lead is Booked, opted out, in an active WhatsApp hold, already contacted for that action, or no longer eligible. The screenshot explicitly says to skip Booked leads; the other checks are operational safeguards. Record suppression and cancel stale queued actions.

05Call within one minute

For an eligible new lead, the AI calling agent reads the current sheet record and attempts the first call within one minute. It speaks Hindi and asks the qualifying questions defined by the business. It must not invent project facts or confirm a booking that has not occurred.

06Retry unanswered leads safely

Record each missed attempt, wait two hours, then recheck the lead before retrying. Allow three total attempts including the first call. After three misses, mark Cold and stop automated calls. If a retry is answered, cancel remaining call retries.

07Qualify and save the call result

Capture project, budget, size, own use versus investment, and timeline using the team's configured questions. Save a concise call summary, outcome, score, attempts, and next action. The screenshots do not define a scoring formula, so agree and configure it before relying on automated scoring.

08Assign one temperature and route

Hot means a visit is booked or buying is expected within 30 days; alert sales the same day. Warm means interested in 1–3 months; start nurture. Cold means three unanswered calls, not interested, or not ready; stop calls. Record why status changed and cancel the old sequence so only one path is active.

09Answer WhatsApp from full history

When the lead messages, the WhatsApp agent reads the full history from the sheet and replies in Hindi or English. The screenshot's 10–20 second response is a target, not a verified guarantee. The agent can answer approved price/size/location/loan questions and share approved photos, brochure, map, or layout. Log the message and action.

10Hold calls while chat is active

Apply the screenshot's 30-minute no-call hold during an active chat. Store a hold-until time and check it immediately before dialing. If the conversation continues, extend the hold. On expiry, recheck status, booking, owner, opt-out, and attempt limit; do not replay stale calls automatically.

11Book a visit or hand off

For a chat booking, record date/time and details, mark Hot, cancel nurture/calls, and alert sales the same day. Send price negotiations or requests for a salesperson to a human with the conversation context. Mark human ownership and record who accepted the handoff and when.

12Run the Hot, Warm, and Cold sequences

Hot is owned by sales. Warm receives WhatsApp follow-ups on days 2, 5, and 10 plus an AI call on the scheduled follow-up date. Cold receives no calls; the screenshot proposes one message a month and possible team revival. Before every action, recheck eligibility and status. A booking promotes Warm/Cold to Hot and cancels the old sequence.

13Log events and alert on errors

Timestamp triggers, attempts, messages, holds, suppressions, bookings, status changes, and handoffs. Send an instant WhatsApp error alert when a step fails, with enough context to find the lead and failed step. Bounded, safe retries should not create duplicate records, messages, or bookings; route unresolved failures to a person.

14Send and reconcile the 1 PM report

Send one daily report at 1 PM covering leads, calls, and site visits. Define the reporting time boundary and whether lead counts mean unique people or submissions. Reconcile totals against the sheet/event log and surface missing updates or unresolved errors; the report alone does not prove every automation succeeded.

15Review results and improve

Review source volume, contact and qualification rates, visits, Hot handoffs, follow-up completion, opt-outs, duplicates, failures, and lead-to-visit conversion. Tune qualifying questions and score criteria based on outcomes. Change one rule at a time, document it, and watch whether lead quality and customer experience improve.

The safeguards and measurement steps extend the screenshot's proposed flow. Confirm actual sheet fields, service connections, message eligibility, time zone, score criteria, and owners before enabling automation.
